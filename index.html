Summary

Alessandro Barbosa holds a degree in Electrical Engineering from Centro Federal de Educação Tecnológica de Minas Gerais (CEFET-MG), a Master of Business Administration from IBMEC-RJ, and is a specialist in Telecommunications by IEC/PUC-MG. He combines business acumen with technical depth, delivering end-to-end solutions from concept to production.

CuidaMais App

React Native mobile application for medication management and treatment adherence. Offline-first system with intelligent notifications, automatic inventory control, price comparison across pharmacies, and gamification to encourage adherence. Event-driven architecture with React Native, Expo, TypeScript, Zustand for global state, local SQLite and multi-device synchronization via Supabase. Native notifications work even with app in background (Android killed state). Features: registration via AI-powered prescription photos, barcode scanning, search or manual entry; automatic tracking of consumed doses; restocking alerts; level system, streaks and rewards; automatic backup and secure synchronization. Backend: REST API built with FastAPI for medication price comparison and prescription processing. Scalable backend with Elasticsearch/OpenSearch for instant search in pharmaceutical product catalog. Main endpoints: dynamic discovery of states/pharmacies, instant database search, suggestions by name/principle/form, item resolution for EANs with ranking/facets, prescription parsing via VLM (Vision Language Model) and heuristic text parsing.
